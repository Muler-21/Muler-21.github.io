RESEARCH

Research interests and output

The genetic and molecular basis of adaptation in livestock, from population genomics to sustainable breeding in ruminants.

Population and evolutionary genomics

Population structure, admixture and demographic history of African cattle.

Environmental adaptation

Genotype–environment interactions, climate resilience and environmental stress responses.

Multi-omics integration

Combining genomic and environmental data to understand adaptive responses in ruminants.

Selection and GWAS

Selection signature detection and genome-wide association for adaptive traits.

Machine learning

ABC-RF for demographic inference, admixture timing and migration.

Sustainable breeding

Bioinformatics and quantitative tools that support climate-smart breeding of ruminants.
