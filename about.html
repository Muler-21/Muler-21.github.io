ABOUT ME

Genomics, adaptation and teaching

Researcher and academic with more than 15 years of experience in university teaching, research and supervision across genetics, breeding, biotechnology and agricultural biochemistry.

Profile

I hold a PhD in Agricultural Technologies and Biotechnologies from the University of Molise (International Doctor Label). My work centers on livestock genomics, population and evolutionary genetics, environmental adaptation, GWAS, genotype-by-environment interaction and selection signature detection.

I apply machine learning, including Approximate Bayesian Computation Random Forest (ABC-RF), to infer demographic history, admixture, migration and adaptive evolution, and I build computational pipelines for large genomic datasets using R, Python, Bash, Linux and HPC.
