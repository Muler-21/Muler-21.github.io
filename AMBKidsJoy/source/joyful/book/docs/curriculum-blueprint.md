# Joyful English Web Book — Curriculum blueprint (Deliverable C)

School grades and CEFR levels are **not equivalent**: the grade column shows where learners *usually* are, and learners should be placed by the Start-here check, not by grade. Completing the stages does not by itself guarantee C2 or native-like proficiency.

## Mapping

| CEFR | Usual grades (Ethiopia) | Joyful English | Stage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pre-A1 | Grades 1–2 | Level 1 | 0–1 |
| A1 | Grades 3–4 | Level 2 | 2 |
| A2 | Grades 5–8 | Levels 3–4 | 2 |
| B1 | Grades 9–10 | Levels 5–6 | 3 |
| B2 | Grades 11–12, university entry | Level 7 | 4 |
| C1 | University, professional | Level 8 | 5 |
| C2 | Specialist | — | 6 |

Pathways: **general** (Stages 0–4), **academic** (Stages 4–6, Purdue OWL, lectures, research writing) and **professional** (the Workplace pathway W.1–W.6, open at A2 to C1).

## Stage 0 · Early English

- **Ages:** about 2–5 · **CEFR:** Pre-A1 (listening first) · **Usual grades:** Pre-school · **Track:** general
- **Goal:** Enjoy English through songs, rhymes, play and everyday words before any reading.
- **Prerequisites:** None.
- **Stage assessment:** Observation: the child joins in songs, follows simple instructions (stand up, touch your nose) and names 30+ objects.

## Stage 1 · Initial literacy

- **Ages:** about 4–7 (by readiness, not age alone) · **CEFR:** Pre-A1 to A1 · **Usual grades:** Grades 1–2 (typical) · **Track:** general
- **Goal:** Connect sounds to letters, blend and decode, read first books and say simple sentences.
- **Prerequisites:** Stage 0 listening and vocabulary, or equivalent.
- **Stage assessment:** Reads 20 sight words, blends three-letter words (c-a-t), reads a short picture book aloud with support.

## Stage 2 · Elementary English

- **Ages:** about 8–14 or adult beginners · **CEFR:** A1–A2 · **Usual grades:** Grades 3–8 (typical) · **Track:** general
- **Goal:** Handle everyday conversations, use present, past and future, read short texts and write paragraphs.
- **Prerequisites:** Can read simple words and sentences (Stage 1).
- **Stage assessment:** A2-style check: understand short dialogues and notices, write a 60-word message, hold a 2-minute conversation about daily life.

- **Stage assessment:** B2-style check: an opinion essay of 250 words, a 5-minute discussion defending a view, a lecture-listening task with notes.

## Stage 6 · Proficiency and specialist English

- **Ages:** adults · **CEFR:** C2-oriented · **Usual grades:** Specialist / professional · **Track:** academic + professional
- **Goal:** Express yourself precisely, edit your own writing, read and synthesise several sources, and interact spontaneously at a high level. Completing this stage does not by itself guarantee C2 or native-like English.
- **Prerequisites:** C1 (Stage 5).
- **Stage assessment:** Portfolio: an edited research article or report, a recorded seminar contribution, and a synthesis of three sources.

## Workplace pathway (all levels)

- **Ages:** job seekers and professionals · **CEFR:** A2 → C1 · **Usual grades:** Graduates and adults · **Track:** professional
- **Goal:** Use English at work: small talk, emails, meetings, interviews, presentations, reports, negotiation and remote teamwork.
- **Prerequisites:** Start at the module that matches your level (A2, B1, B2 or C1).
- **Stage assessment:** Workplace portfolio: CV and cover letter, three professional emails, a recorded interview answer and a 5-minute presentation.
