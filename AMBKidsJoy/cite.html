AMBKidsJoy (the learning hub, its 3D lesson videos and its songs) is the original work of Mulusew Kassa Bitew. If you use it in teaching, research, a report or your own materials, please cite it as shown below. Original workኦሪጅናል ሥራ

The characters (Kokeb the star, Saba, Dawit and the lion, gelada, walia ibex, cow and chicken), the 3D scenes, the song lyrics and the music are original to AMBKidsJoy. The music is generated from code with no samples or outside recordings. No footage, characters or songs from other channels are used.ገጸ-ባሕርያቱ (ኮከብ፣ ሳባ፣ ዳዊትና አንበሳ፣ ጭላዳ፣ ዋልያ፣ ላምና ዶሮ)፣ የ3D ትዕይንቶቹ፣ የመዝሙሮቹ ግጥምና ሙዚቃው የAMBKidsJoy ኦሪጅናል ሥራዎች ናቸው። ሙዚቃው ያለ ናሙና ወይም የሌላ ቀረጻ በኮድ የተሠራ ነው። ከሌሎች ቻናሎች ምንም ቪዲዮ፣ ገጸ-ባሕርይ ወይም መዝሙር አልተወሰደም።
